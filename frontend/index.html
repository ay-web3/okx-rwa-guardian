the problem

DeFi can't see the physical world

While today's oracle networks deliver verified data like prices, smart contracts cannot interpret complex real-world events. A protocol may receive seismic data after an earthquake, but it can't synthesize news and geological reports to determine if a tokenized property is actually destroyed. RWA Guardian bridges the gap between raw data and actionable risk intelligence.

✕ Without RWA Guardian

A 7.5-magnitude earthquake strikes the Noto Peninsula. The oracle reports the event, but the smart contract cannot assess the property's risk or determine the appropriate response.

✓ With RWA Guardian

Our AI swarm detects the earthquake from USGS, cross-references local news and economic data, and returns a signed decision: raiseCollateralRatio. The lending protocol tightens requirements automatically — within seconds.

autonomous reasoning

Dynamic Risk Weighting

RWA Guardian doesn't rely on rigid mathematical formulas. The AI acts autonomously, dynamically weighting threat factors to mirror human intelligence during crisis events.

Rigid Logic (Traditional)

Risk = (Physical × 0.5) + (Economic × 0.5)

A massive 7.0 magnitude earthquake occurs (Physical Risk = 100). The local economy is completely stable (Economic Risk = 0). A rigid formula blindly averages them, resulting in a Risk Score of 50. The catastrophic physical danger is dangerously diluted.

Dynamic Weights (RWA Guardian)

Risk = (Physical × 1.0) + (Economic × 0.0)

The AI evaluates the same earthquake and recognizes the catastrophic physical threat. It autonomously overrides the default weights, assigning 100% weight to Physical Risk. The final score correctly spikes to 100.
